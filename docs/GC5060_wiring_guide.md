# GC5060 Electrical Wiring Installation Guide

This guide provides technical instructions for the safe and compliant electrical wiring installation of the GC5060 Generator System, addressing specific inquiries raised by United Oil & Gas Corp.

## 1. Safety Specifications
- Ensure all power sources are disconnected before starting the wiring installation.
- Follow local electrical codes (NEC / IEC).

## 2. Wiring Diagram
- Connect the primary feed to terminals L1, L2, and L3 (3-Phase 480V Delta).
- Neutral wire must be grounded at the main distribution panel.

## 3. Post-Installation Testing
- Verify phase-to-phase voltages before energizing the system.
- Ensure current CSAT on support inquiries is maintained by resolving these guides within SLA limits.
